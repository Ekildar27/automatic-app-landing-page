Privacy Policy — NexusNote

1. Who we are

Ekildar Office (“we,” “us,” or “our”) operates the NexusNote application (“the App” or “the Service”). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what data is involved, how it is collected, all purposes for which it is used, with whom it may be shared, and how we obtain your permission before sharing.

By using the App, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy. It should be read together with our Terms of Use.

2. Core principles

  • No account required. You can use the App without creating an account with us.
  • Local-first. Notes, attachments, tags, and related metadata are stored on your device by default (including the App Group container used by the Share Extension on the same device).
  • We do not operate a NexusNote backend that receives your note contents. Ekildar Office does not run a cloud server that stores your notes for normal use.
  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • No advertising or analytics SDKs in the current release.
  • Optional outbound sharing only with your action and, for third-party AI, your explicit prior consent.

7. AI Organizer consent flow (detail)

AI Organizer is optional and Pro-gated. NexusNote does not sell AI quota and does not operate its own AI server that receives Organizer note content.

Before any note content or connection probe is sent to a third-party AI provider, the App presents a dedicated Consent Sheet that:

  1. Names the recipient (Groq, OpenRouter, or DeepSeek—whichever you selected);
  2. Lists what is sent (title, text, privacy-filtered URL if any);
  3. Lists what is not sent (photos, audio, video, attachments, other notes);
  4. States the purpose (title / summary / tags for the current note only);
  5. Offers Allow sharing with [Provider] and Don’t Allow;
  6. Links to this Privacy Policy.

Changing provider requires a new Allow. Revoking consent stops further outbound Organizer requests.

8. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have received such information on our systems, contact us and we will delete it where applicable. (Note contents are not stored on Ekildar Office servers in normal use.)

9. Security

We take commercially reasonable steps to protect information under our control. No method of electronic storage or internet transmission is 100% secure. For optional iCloud features, Apple’s security model applies. For AI Organizer, transmission uses HTTPS to the provider you chose; your API key remains under your control on device (and with that provider per their terms).

10. Links to other sites

The App or this Policy may link to third-party sites (for example provider privacy pages). We do not control those sites and are not responsible for their content or practices. Review their policies separately.

11. International users

Notes remain on your device by default. Optional iCloud backup/sync may involve Apple storing or transferring data per Apple’s iCloud policies and your Apple ID region. Optional AI Organizer (after consent) sends the Organizer payload to your chosen provider, which may process data in regions where that provider operates. Ekildar Office does not transfer your note contents to Ekildar Office servers as part of normal App use.

12. Changes

We may update this Policy when features change. Material changes take effect when posted here with an updated effective date. We may also notify you in the App when appropriate. Continued use after an update means you acknowledge the revised Policy.
