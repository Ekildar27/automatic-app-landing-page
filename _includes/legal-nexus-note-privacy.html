Privacy Policy — NexusNote

1. Who we are

NexusNote (“the App”) is a private notes application published by Ekildar Office. This Privacy Policy explains what data is involved when you use the App and how it is handled.

2. Summary

  • We do not require an account to use the App.
  • Your notes, attachments, tags, and related metadata are stored on your device (including the App Group container used by the Share Extension on the same device).
  • In the current release, we do not operate a NexusNote cloud backend that receives your notes.
  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • The current release does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • Apple may process App Store purchase, download, and crash diagnostics under Apple’s own policies when you use the App Store / Apple systems. That processing is controlled by Apple, not by Ekildar Office’s note storage.

3. Data stored on your device

Depending on how you use the App, the following may be stored locally on your device:

  • Note text, titles, tags, spaces, and timestamps
  • Links (URLs) you save
  • Photos, videos, and voice recordings you attach
  • App preferences (for example appearance and language)

This data remains under your control on the device unless you:

  • export or share it yourself;
  • back up the device with your own tools (for example an encrypted device backup); or
  • send content through the system Share sheet to another app or service you choose.

4. Permissions

The App may request the following iOS permissions only when you use related features:

PermissionPurpose
Photo LibraryOnly when you choose to attach images to a note
MicrophoneOnly when you choose to record a voice note

You can revoke permissions in iOS Settings. Doing so may disable the related features.

5. Share Extension

When you save content from another app via the Share Extension, that content is written into the local App Group storage used by NexusNote on your device. We do not transmit that content to Ekildar Office servers.

6. Clipboard

If you use copy actions in the App, text may be placed on the system pasteboard so you can paste it elsewhere. The App does not send clipboard contents to our servers.

7. Purchases (if offered)

If the App offers optional paid features (for example subscriptions), payment is processed by Apple. We do not receive your full payment card details. Apple may provide us with limited purchase status information needed to unlock features on your device, subject to Apple’s rules.

8. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. Do not use the App to store data if you are not permitted to do so under applicable law.

9. International users

Because notes stay on your device in the current release, Ekildar Office does not transfer your note contents to our servers as part of normal App use. If you later enable a future sync or cloud feature (if offered), we will update this policy before that feature processes personal data off-device.

10. Changes

We may update this policy when features change. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date on this page. Continued use of the App after an update means you acknowledge the revised policy.

隐私政策 — 知仓

1. 我们是谁

知仓（NexusNote，以下简称「本应用」）由 Ekildar Office 发布。本隐私政策说明您使用本应用时涉及哪些数据，以及这些数据如何被处理。

2. 概要

  • 使用本应用无需注册账号。
  • 您的笔记、附件、标签及相关元数据保存在您的设备本地（含同一设备上分享扩展使用的 App Group 容器）。
  • 当前版本没有接收您笔记内容的知仓云端服务器。
  • 我们不会出售您的个人数据。
  • 当前版本未集成第三方广告或统计分析 SDK。
  • 当您通过 App Store / Apple 系统下载、购买或产生崩溃诊断时，Apple 可能按其自身政策处理相关数据；该部分由 Apple 控制，不属于知仓对本机笔记的存储。

3. 设备本地数据

根据使用方式，本应用可能在设备本地保存：

  • 笔记正文、标题、标签、主题空间与时间戳
  • 您保存的链接（URL）
  • 您添加的图片、视频与语音
  • 应用偏好（如外观、语言等）

除非您自行导出/分享、使用您自己的设备备份工具，或通过系统分享面板发送到其他应用/服务，否则上述数据由您在本机控制。

4. 系统权限

仅在您使用相关功能时，本应用可能请求：

权限用途
相册仅在您主动为笔记添加图片时使用
麦克风仅在您主动录制语音时使用

您可在 iOS「设置」中随时关闭权限；关闭后相关功能可能不可用。

5. 分享扩展

通过系统分享从其他 App 存入的内容，会写入本机 App Group，不会上传到 Ekildar Office 服务器。

6. 剪贴板

若您在应用内使用复制，文本可能进入系统剪贴板以便粘贴到别处。本应用不会把剪贴板内容发送到我们的服务器。

7. 付费功能（如有）

若本应用提供可选付费功能（例如订阅），付款由 Apple 处理。我们不会获取您的完整银行卡信息。Apple 可能在其规则允许范围内，向我们提供用于在本机解锁功能所需的有限购买状态信息。

8. 儿童

本应用不以 13 岁以下儿童为主要服务对象。若适用法律不允许您存储相关数据，请勿使用本应用进行存储。

9. 跨境说明

当前版本笔记保存在您的设备本地，正常使用时 Ekildar Office 不会把您的笔记内容传输到我们的服务器。若未来提供同步或云端能力，我们将在该功能处理离机个人数据前更新本政策。

10. 政策变更

功能变化时我们可能更新本政策，并以更新日期为准。更新后继续使用本应用，即表示您知悉修订后的政策。
